HDFS log anomaly detection at scale

DOMAINMachine learning · Security
DATA11M+ log lines

Finding suspicious behavior in millions of HDFS log lines is difficult to do by hand.

Train and tune an anomaly detection model, then make its output usable through a Streamlit app.

$ cat FLOODCAST.model

FloodCast.

02 / 04

Monthly flood prediction for DKI Jakarta

MODELRandom Forest
VALIDATIONChronological holdout
PLATFORMFlask

PROBLEM

Flood prediction depends on weather patterns that change over time, including rainfall and seasonality.

APPROACH

Use rainfall, seasonal features, and lagged climate indicators in a Random Forest model.

RESULT

AUC 0.724 and F1 0.687 on a chronological holdout.

// THE WORK

I trained and tuned the model, and contributed to the frontend and backend of the Flask application. The evaluation uses a chronological holdout so later months are tested after earlier ones.

The project turns climate indicators into a monthly prediction for DKI Jakarta.
