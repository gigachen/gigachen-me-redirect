02 / About

Curiosity, with a method.

I like the moment an unfamiliar system starts to make sense: a log pattern, a hidden file, a piece of code that behaves differently than expected.

In CTFs with PETIR Cyber Security, I follow evidence through digital forensics and reverse engineering challenges. Working with teammates has taught me to explain what I find as clearly as I investigate it.

That same habit shapes the tools I build: take a messy problem, test an approach, and make the result useful to someone else.

I am based in Jakarta and study Computer Science at Bina Nusantara University.
