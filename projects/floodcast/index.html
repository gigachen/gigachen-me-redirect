Project / Applied machine learning

FloodCast

Monthly flood prediction for DKI Jakarta using rainfall and climate indicators.

  • ModelRandom Forest
  • ValidationChronological holdout
  • ResultAUC 0.724 · F1 0.687
Climate indicators→Random Forest→Monthly prediction

Inputs include rainfall, seasonal features, and lagged climate indicators.

The problem

Flood prediction depends on weather patterns that change over time, including rainfall and seasonality.

My contribution

I trained and tuned the Random Forest model, helped build the Flask app, and evaluated the model on a chronological holdout.

Outcome

The model reached AUC 0.724 and F1 0.687 on the chronological holdout.
